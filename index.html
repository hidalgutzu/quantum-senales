      What is QUANTUM Signals?

      QUANTUM Signals is a free quantitative crypto tool. It turns every candle of Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP and Cardano into 11 measurable factors, finds the most similar moments in each market's history and estimates the probability that a trade reaches its target before its stop. It only says BUY or SELL when that probability beats break-even with margin and the model has proven itself on data it never saw.

      Probabilities come from past data and do not guarantee future results. Always use the stop. Not financial advice.
